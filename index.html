Dr. Ali Hagverdiyev

Lecturer, Department of Mathematics • University of Miami

Bio

Dr. Hagverdiyev earned his Bachelor’s degree in Applied Mathematics from Baku State University in Azerbaijan, where his high entrance exam score earned him both a tuition waiver and a stipend. After receiving a competitive scholarship through the Azerbaijani Ministry of Education's Study Abroad Program, he continued his education at the University of Nottingham, earning a Master’s degree in Pure Mathematics. He later went on to earn both a Master’s degree and a PhD in Applied Mathematics from the Florida Institute of Technology.

In addition to his academic achievements, Dr. Hagverdiyev brings extensive teaching experience to his role. While completing his studies, he served as a teaching assistant at Texas A&M University and the Florida Institute of Technology, worked as a part-time lecturer at Keiser University, and tutored online for Holyoke Community College. Following his PhD, he joined Springdale Preparatory School as a full-time teacher, where he also earned his International Baccalaureate (IB) Category 1 certification in Mathematics: Applications and Interpretation. Since 2011, Dr. Hagverdiyev has served as a full-time lecturer at the University of Miami.
